Work

Everything else I've built.

Sorted by what each one catches. The habit is the same every time: find the failure before it costs someone.

  1. a deepfake face
    DeepSight AI

    An EfficientNet-B3 model, 98.6% accurate, with Grad-CAM showing where it looked.

    National winner, NexGen Hackathon 2025. Top team of 6,000.
  2. disease before symptoms
    Project AEGIS

    A walk-through chamber of thermal, millimetre-wave, ultrasound, low-dose X-ray, breath and gait sensors. I designed the hardware and sensor suite; Vikashini S built the software.

    Finalist, L&T TECHgium.
  3. a gearbox about to resonate
    Modal analysis, DRDO CVRDE

    Natural frequencies and mode shapes in ANSYS, for an aircraft-mounted gearbox.

  4. a cracked propeller
    Propeller NDT

    A phone's accelerometer and microphone, read through FFT. No lab equipment needed.

    Open source.
  5. illness in a voice
    Timbre

    88 voice biomarkers and a Random Forest, 92.5% accurate, with SHAP explanations. Three seconds of "ahhh".

    Open source.
  6. a dangerous route
    SafetyNav

    Crime-data heatmaps and route scoring.

    GDG on Campus Solution Challenge 2025.
